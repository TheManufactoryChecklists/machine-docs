# Grizzly MODEL G0453PX POLAR BEAR SERIES 15" PLANER w/SPIRAL CUTTERHEAD

**Room:** WoodShop                   <span style="float:right;">**Date:** 2026Mar12</span>

**Training Required:** YES — Dimensioning Lumber

**PPE Required:** Safety glasses, hearing protection

Review these prior to use, and to maintain familiarity as needed

## Safety

- **Never operate without required training** - Read OEM user manual (link below)
- **Stay alert** — no distractions, no loose clothing, no gloves near rotating tools
- **Inspect the machine before use** — damage, debris inside, missing guards, loose parts
- Report issues immediately
- **Emergency stop** Test it
- This machine can pull you in - remain clear of entry
- Fingers can be pinched under wood when engaging - remain clear of table

## Limitations

- **DO NOT CHANGE SPEEDS** unless **ON**
- **Material limits:**
  - Max Width; 15"
  - Max Thickness; 8"
  - Min Thickness; 0.375" (3/8")
  - Min Length; 8"
  - Max Cut Depth; 1/16" (half-turn per pass)
- **Recommended speeds/feeds:**
  - Two speeds available (3 or 6 inches/sec)
  - Try slower speed first (push in while machine is **ON**)
  - Thermal overload will trip if too fast or deep

## Maintenence

- Empty garbage can (dumpster out back) if >75% full

## Pre‑Use Checklist

- Verify wood is free of metal (no staples, screws, nails, etc.)
- No obstacles at entry or exit (tripping hazard)
- Table free of debris, including inside
- Nothing hanging down inside
- Open suction gate
- Verify hose is clear
- Guards, shields, and safety devices in place

## Operating Procedures

1. Raise depth of cut slightly above wood thickness
2. Turn machine on, then set speed
3. Verify suction
4. Slowly push wood in, while supporting trailing edge
5. If not cutting, slowly turn depth handle CW (raises table) while wood is moving
    - Stop turning handle once cutting begins
6. After removal, 1/2 turn CW pfor next pass (note depth on scale)
7. Flip wood as desired

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Too deep, or too fast, or both
- **DO NOT** push wood
- Reaching across moving parts
- Leaving the machine running unattended
- Using the machine while tired or distracted

## Post‑Use Responsibilities

- Press E-stop and wait for full stop
- Clean the machine and surrounding area with compressed air
- Empty garbage can (if required)

## Emergency Procedures

- **Emergency stop:** If wood is trapped, unplug, then lower table
- **First aid kit:** Sink outside men's restroom

Solution 003: Thermal overload tripped (cutting too deep o/ fast), so wait 10 minutes for it to cool.
